## Validation and coverage

Commands and actual results; checks not run and why; paths inspected, partial
coverage and untouched areas. Do not include fabricated measurements or pass claims.

## Uncertainties and decisions

Open questions that could change the verdict; disagreements; material dismissed
findings and counterevidence. Optional improvements are nonblocking and separate.

## Production gate

Include for gate/audit or launch-readiness requests. Show applicable controls,
status and evidence, overall result, and actionable handoffs for unknowns/failures.
